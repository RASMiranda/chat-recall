# Privacy Policy — Chat Recall

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Chat Recall searches your own claude.ai chat history on your own device.

**What it reads.** While you are signed in to claude.ai, the extension requests your chat list and chat contents from claude.ai itself, using your existing browser session.

**Where it is stored.** A search index of that text is stored only in your browser's local extension storage. It never leaves your device.

**What it sends.** Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no requests except to `https://claude.ai`. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no remote code and no advertising.

**Donation link.** The toolbar popup may show a link to a donation page. It is a normal link: nothing is loaded or sent unless you click it.

**Permissions.** `storage` and `unlimitedStorage` (to keep the local index); access to `https://claude.ai/*` (to read your chats and show the search box).

**Your control.** "Clear index" in the toolbar popup deletes the stored index. Removing the extension deletes all its data.

**Contact.** Questions or concerns: https://github.com/RASMiranda/chat-recall/issues
